Monty Hall Simulator

One door hides a car. The other two hide goats. Pick a door, the host opens a goat door, then you decide: stay or switch.

The computer plays both strategies many times so you can see what really happens.

Why does switching win?

The host knows where the car is and always opens goat doors, so your first pick is still only a 1 in 3 guess. The other 2 in 3 chance gets squeezed onto the one door left closed.

So switching wins 2 times out of 3.

Try it with 100 doors. You pick one, a 1 in 100 chance. The host opens 98 goat doors and leaves one closed. Your door is still a 1 in 100 guess, so the other door almost certainly has the car.
